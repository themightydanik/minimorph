## Controls

Desktop uses WASD/arrows, Space for Jump, either Shift for Dive, Q/E for the existing camera. Touch uses a fixed-origin 360-degree stick and separate Jump/Dive buttons. Radial dead zone is 14%; displacement outside it continuously maps to movement strength and saturates at the unit circle. Diagonal input cannot exceed full movement speed. One captured pointer owns the stick, leaving other fingers available for actions/camera. Pointer up/cancel/lost capture, blur, visibility changes, menu transitions and disposal clear input. The knob follows displacement immediately; acceleration smooths actual velocity. Android browser behavior is exercised with Chromium touch emulation; a physical Android comfort/latency pass remains useful.

## Shared movement

Normal movement retains 6 units/s top speed, 34 units/s² acceleration, 42 units/s² deceleration, existing per-arena airborne control, jump height/gravity, coyote time and jump buffering. Jump is edge-triggered, so holding a raw input cannot cause automatic landing jumps. Ordinary race/training routes remain usable without Dive.

`normal → airborne Jump → dive → slide → recover → normal` is owned by `game-simulation`, using the existing 120 Hz fixed step. Dive is also allowed from the ground, entering Slide directly. It sets forward speed to at most 8.4 units/s (never adds boosts), follows the camera-relative stick direction or current heading, and clamps vertical velocity downward to at most −1 units/s; it cannot act as a second jump. Airborne dive steering is limited to 18% control with no repeated thrust. Landing enters a 0.42 s friction slide, followed by 0.22 s recovery. Jump and Dive are locked during these actions. A Dive press is eligible only if the fixed step **began** in Normal; a press on the exact Slide/Recovery expiry step is consumed, never buffered into a new Dive. Holding Dive through any action transition or cooldown expiry requires release and a new press before another Dive. Cooldown starts at 1.1 s on an accepted Dive and otherwise only counts down; landing, interruption, pad launch and checkpoint respawn never clear or restart it. Ordinary airborne steering stays responsive.

Existing obstacle collisions still apply. Knockback interrupts the action; checkpoint respawn resets it without clearing the remaining cooldown. Jump pads/bounce platforms retain their launch behavior and interrupt the temporary action. The `diveUsed` token stays spent through Slide/Recovery and airborne interruptions. It rearms only on grounded Normal (including the step Recovery finishes while grounded) or actual checkpoint restoration, not when respawn begins. A pad launch never resets it directly: normal grounded contact before launch can rearm it, whereas interrupting Slide/Recovery with a pad keeps it spent until the next normal landing or checkpoint restoration. No transition resets the held-input latch. No smaller collision body or hazard immunity is granted by the slide pose. Timer, checkpoints, race finish/results, rooms and reconnect remain owned by their existing systems.

## Multiplayer and presentation

The authoritative server and prediction/reconciliation use the same simulation and the same four substeps per 30 Hz input command. **Wire protocol is v2** because the shared movement state/physics contract changed. Deploy server and clients from this commit together; v1 is rejected rather than running mismatched prediction. Existing match reconnect behavior is retained within v2; server restarts still expire in-memory rooms as before. The optional boolean `dive` is a held level across all four substeps and consecutive commands, defaulting to false. Keyboard/touch retain physical hold and queue short taps; no artificial substep release can manufacture new edges. A missing command retains the last level until a new/neutral command, disconnect or the existing stale-input timeout; arbitrary extra fields are rejected. Dive intent is validated at the protocol boundary; clients never submit action state, position or velocity. Snapshots include action/timers/cooldown and remotes present the authoritative action. Bot navigation observes action locks; hard bots may dive only while descending into a sufficiently long clear landing deck. Existing geometry-aware jumping, gates, checkpoints and all three full rounds remain covered.

Only the six supplied animation clips are used. Normal Idle/Run/Jump/Fall/Land/Victory behavior remains. Dive/Slide conservatively reuse Fall with a smoothed root pitch (25°/65° forward); Recovery uses Land with 0.12 s clip blending. No new skeletal animation or silhouette scaling was introduced. **Dedicated Dive, belly-slide and get-up clips are still required for final art quality.** The temporary tilted Fall pose has imperfect limb contact and may intersect the deck during a transition. It does not change collision geometry or the simulation. Prior accepted skinning defects remain.

Arena geometry, scenery, lights, environmental materials/effects, rendering resolution/DPR and automatic quality policy are unchanged.
